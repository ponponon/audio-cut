隐私政策

适用应用：本地音剪（Audio Cut） · 最近更新：2026 年 10 月 30 日

本政策说明本地音剪如何处理你在 Android 或 iOS 设备上主动选择的音频文件。本地音剪由本地音剪项目维护者（GitHub：ponponon）开发。

音频文件如何处理

你通过系统文件选择器选中的音频，只用于在当前设备上读取媒体信息、生成波形、试听、剪切和转码。应用会把工作副本、波形和导出文件写入应用自己的临时目录；应用下次启动时会清理该工作目录。应用不会修改你选中的原始文件。

收集、上传与第三方服务

本地音剪不创建账号，不提供云同步、广告、远程分析或崩溃上报。应用不把音频、文件名、设备标识或使用记录发送给本地音剪的维护者或其服务器。音频处理在设备本机完成；应用不申请麦克风权限，只通过系统文件选择器访问你主动选中的文件。Android 10 及以上版本保存导出文件不需要任何存储权限；Android 9 及以下版本仅在保存导出文件时申请存储权限。

导出、保存与分享

导出时，你可以选择「保存到设备」把文件写入本机 Music/AudioCut 文件夹（iPhone 上保存到「文件」App 中的应用文件夹），或交给系统分享面板。只有你主动选择接收应用或保存位置后，文件才会交给该目标。如果你选择云盘、即时通信或其他第三方应用，目标应用可能按其自己的条款和隐私政策上传、保存或处理文件；本地音剪无法控制该目标的行为。

保存期限与删除

应用临时工作目录会在下次启动时清理；「保存到设备」写入公共文件夹的副本不会被应用清理。你也可以通过系统设置清除本地音剪的应用数据，或卸载应用，以移除应用保留在本机的文件。系统可能在此之前回收临时文件。你主动交给其他应用的副本，需要在对应应用中单独管理。

儿童隐私

本地音剪不面向儿童提供专门内容，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。应用不收集或传输个人数据。

政策变更

如果应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面和应用内的隐私说明，并注明更新日期。

联系维护者

隐私问题可通过 GitHub 项目问题反馈页 联系维护者。请不要在公开问题中附上私人音频、个人信息或其他敏感材料。

Privacy policy in English

This policy explains how Local Audio Cut handles audio files that you choose on an Android or iOS device. The app is developed by the Local Audio Cut project maintainer (GitHub: ponponon).

How audio files are handled

Audio selected by you through the system file picker is used only to read media information, generate a waveform, preview, trim, and transcode it on the current device. The app stores working copies, waveforms, and exports in its private temporary directory and clears that directory on the next launch. The app does not modify the original file.

Collection, uploads, and third-party services

Local Audio Cut has no accounts, cloud sync, ads, remote analytics, or crash reporting. The app does not send audio, file names, device identifiers, or usage records to the project maintainer or its servers. Audio processing happens on the device. The app does not request microphone access; it uses the system file picker to access files that you select. On Android 10 and later, saving an export needs no storage permission at all. On Android 9 and earlier, a storage permission is requested only when you save an export.

Export, saving, and sharing

When you export, you can save the file to this device's Music/AudioCut folder (on iPhone, to the app folder in the Files app), or hand it to the system share sheet. The file is handed to a destination only after you choose a receiving app or save location. If you choose a cloud drive, messaging app, or another third-party app, that destination may upload, retain, or process the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Local Audio Cut does not control the destination's behavior.

Retention and deletion

The app clears its temporary working directory on the next launch. Copies saved to the device's public folder are not cleared by the app. You can also clear Local Audio Cut's data in system settings or uninstall the app to remove files retained on the device. The operating system may remove temporary files sooner. Copies that you choose to give to other apps must be managed in those apps.

Children's privacy

Local Audio Cut does not provide content specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. The app does not collect or transmit personal data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, we will update this page and the in-app privacy information and show the updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the GitHub project issue page. Please do not include private audio, personal information, or other sensitive material in a public issue.
