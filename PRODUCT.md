# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want to trim audio from a personal device library and care that their recordings stay private. They may be using a phone and need a quick, familiar way to choose a file, set a precise range, listen, and save a result.

## Product Purpose

本地音剪 is a cross-platform Android and iOS audio editor. It imports a file selected by the user, previews its waveform, lets the user choose exact in/out points, auditions the selection, and exports a common audio format. Audio decoding and editing happen on the device; the app has no account, automatic cloud sync, or audio upload.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel as familiar and direct as iOS Voice Memos while making the local-only promise clear without turning privacy into a marketing banner.

## Anti-references

- The previous interface's near-black canvas paired with mint-green oversized actions, stacked oversized rounded cards, and a slogan-like hero heading.
- Audio editors that require an account, automatically synchronize recordings, or send audio to a remote service.
- Decorative visual effects that make the waveform and trim boundaries harder to see.

## Design Principles

- Keep audio and edit operations on the device, and explain that plainly.
- Make the waveform and selected time range the visual center of the editor.
- Use familiar platform controls and clear feedback for import, playback, time entry, and export.
- Keep the surface calm and light, with one restrained accent for active controls and selection.
- Follow system dynamic type, maintain readable contrast, and provide generous touch targets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support the system's dynamic text size without clipping controls. Maintain WCAG AA contrast for text, keep the range handles and time fields clearly distinguishable, and provide touch targets of at least 44 logical points where practical.
